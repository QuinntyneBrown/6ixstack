# 12 · Share event

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-event

Share an event when you want friends or teammates to come along. Start on the event's page.

## 02-share-dialog

Choose Share. You get a short link to the event, ready to copy, plus one-tap options for LinkedIn, Bluesky and
email. On a phone, your own share sheet opens instead.

## 03-link-copied

Copy the link and the button simply says Copied. Paste it anywhere, and it takes people straight to the event.
One link, one tap, and the word is out.
