# 04 · Submit an event

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-open-submit

Submit event lets you put your meetup or talk in front of developers across the GTA, free, with no account
needed. Choose Submit event at the top of any page, and the form opens.

## 02-error-summary

If you send it too early, nothing is lost. A short list at the top says exactly what's missing, and each item
takes you straight to the field that needs it.

## 03-streaming-fields

Pick how people join. Choose Streaming and the venue and address fields step aside, leaving just a spot for your
livestream link. Hybrid shows both.

## 04-ready-to-submit

Add the price and a ticket link if you have one, then your email. Only moderators see it. The form also keeps
a draft as you type, so you can come back later.

## 05-in-review

That's it. Your event is in review, and you get a confirmation by email. It stays out of the public list until a
moderator has checked it.

## 06-verification-email

The first time you submit, we also send a link to confirm your email address. Here it is, arriving in the
development mailbox.

## 07-email-verified

Open the link and you're verified, so anything a moderator approves goes live straight away. Submit, confirm,
and your event is on its way to the 6ix.
