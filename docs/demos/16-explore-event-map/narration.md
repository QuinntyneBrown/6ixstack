# 16 · Explore event map

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-map-beside-the-list

The event map shows you where everything is happening. Right beside the list, every meetup in the GTA is a dot
in its topic's colour, and streams arrive in Toronto as arcs from around the world.

## 02-hover-a-row

Point at an event in the list and its dot lights up on the map, with a little card showing the title and time.
Click a dot, and the list jumps straight to that event.

## 03-pick-a-day

Pick a day on the timeline to see just that day. The map narrows down, and the other days in the list fade back
so the one you chose stands out.

## 04-play-the-next-two-weeks

Or press Play, and the map steps through the next two weeks, one busy day at a time.

## 05-phone-map-above-the-list

On your phone, the map sits right above the list. See where it's all happening, at a glance.
