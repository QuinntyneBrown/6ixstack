## 01-directory

Every community on 6ixstack has its own page. Start in the directory and choose a group, like GTA Azure Community.

## 02-community-profile

You get the whole picture: what the group is about, how many people follow it, how often it meets and where, its
website and links, and the people who run it.

## 03-upcoming-and-past

Below that are its upcoming events, and its most recent past ones, with how many people came and a recap of each.

## 04-event

Pick an event to see everything about it. Get to know a community before you show up.
