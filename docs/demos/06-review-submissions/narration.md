# 06 · Review submissions

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-signed-in-moderator

Review submissions is where moderators keep 6ixstack trustworthy. Signed in as a moderator, your initials in the
header take you straight to the review queue.

## 02-review-queue

Every event waiting for a look is here, oldest first. The line at the top tells you how many are waiting, how
long the oldest has been there, and how quickly reviews usually happen.

## 03-automated-checks

Each card shows what was submitted and what 6ixstack already checked for you: whether the links work, whether the
address can be found, whether it looks like a duplicate, and whether the sender has a track record. Warnings
stand out, so you know where to look closer.

## 04-keyboard-next

You can move through the queue without the mouse. Press J for the next submission and K to go back.

## 05-shortcuts-list

Press the question mark any time to see the shortcuts. They never fire while you're typing.

## 06-organizer-forbidden

Organizers who open the queue get a clear explanation, a way back to their events, and a way to ask to help.
Review submissions: a calm, checked queue, one card at a time.
