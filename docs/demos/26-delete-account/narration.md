## 01-settings

If you're done organizing on 6ixstack, you can delete your account yourself. It's at the bottom of Settings.

## 02-delete-account

6ixstack tells you exactly what happens: you lose access, and your published events stay up for everyone who
planned to go.

## 03-type-to-confirm

To make sure it's really you, type your email. Only then can you delete.

## 04-signed-out

Your account and personal details are gone, you're signed out everywhere, and you're back on the events page.
Your data, your call.
