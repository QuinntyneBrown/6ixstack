# 01 · Browse events

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-events-by-day

Browse events lets you see every developer event in Toronto, the GTA and online for the next two weeks, without
signing in. Events are grouped by day, in Toronto time, and each row shows where it is, its topics, the price and
how many people are going.

## 02-events-on-a-phone

On your phone it's the same list in a single column, with the filters right above it.

## 03-in-the-gta

Only want to go somewhere in person? Choose In the GTA. The list narrows to nine events, and the counts on each
option tell you what you'd see before you tap.

## 04-topic-ai

Pick a topic, like AI, and only those events stay. You can pick more than one, and Clear filters puts everything
back.

## 05-search

Then search for anything: a talk, a group, or a neighbourhood. Searching for Mississauga finds the Azure Data and
AI Day.

## 06-shared-link

Your filters live in the address bar. Send the link to a friend, or open it in a new tab, and the same list comes
back. 6ixstack also remembers your topic and format for next time.

## 07-no-results

If nothing matches, you get a friendly message instead of a blank page, with a quick way to clear your filters
or get a calendar feed for that search.

## 08-more-events

And when two weeks isn't enough, show twenty more and keep scrolling into the rest of the month. That's Browse
events: every dev event in the 6ix, filtered your way.
