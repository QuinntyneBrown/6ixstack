# 00 · The 6ixstack app shell

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-events-light

The 6ixstack app shell is the frame around every page. It gives you the brand, the main places to go, a button
to submit an event, and a footer with the site pages. You can always see where you are, and the Events link is
marked as the page you're on.

## 02-events-dark

If your computer is set to dark mode, 6ixstack follows it. Every colour comes from the same design tokens, so the
whole site switches at once and the text stays easy to read.

## 03-mobile-menu

On a phone, the links fold into a menu button. Tap it and a sheet slides up from the bottom with every
destination, plus calendar feeds, organizer sign in, and a big Submit event button. Press Escape and you land
right back on the menu button.

## 04-not-found

Follow a broken link and you won't hit a dead end. A friendly page says we can't find it, and offers to take you
back to the events or open the calendar.

## 05-toasts

When something finishes, a short message pops up in the corner. Most of them fade away after eight seconds, but
they wait while you point at them, and anything that went wrong stays until you close it.

## 06-offline-banner

Lose your connection, and a banner under the header tells you straight away. Submitting an event is paused, so
you don't lose your work.

## 07-back-online-banner

When the connection comes back, the banner turns green and tells you everything is up to date. That's the
6ixstack app shell: one clear frame, in light or dark, on any screen.
