# 02 · View event detail

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-pick-from-the-list

View event detail lets you open any event and see everything you need to decide whether to go. Start from the
list and pick one, like the Azure Data and AI Day.

## 02-event-detail

Up top you get the date, the title and the community running it. On the right, the facts: the day and time in
Toronto, the venue with directions, whether there's a livestream, the price, and how many people are going.

## 03-agenda-and-speakers

Scroll down for the full description, the agenda in order, and the speakers, each linking to their own page.
When you're ready, tickets open on the organizer's site. 6ixstack never charges you.

## 04-streaming-event

A streaming event shows its time in Toronto and where it's hosted. Before it starts, a note tells you the stream
link will appear here fifteen minutes before, and it does, without a refresh.

## 05-past-event

For an event that's already happened, you'll see that it ended, how many attended, and links to the recording
and the slides, plus the community's next event.

## 06-cancelled-event

If an organizer cancels, that's the first thing you see, with their reason. Instead of tickets, you get a quick
way to find another event on the same topics.

## 07-docked-ticket-bar

On your phone, the date, price and ticket button stay docked at the bottom as you read. That's View event detail:
everything about an event, on one clear page.
