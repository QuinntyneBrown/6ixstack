# 20 · Receive email notifications

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-approve-a-submission

When a moderator decides on your event, you hear about it by email. Here, a moderator approves Fabric for Power BI
developers.

## 02-approved-email

A few seconds later, once the chance to undo has passed, the organizer gets this: their event is live, with a link
to see it. It also shows up in their notifications on 6ixstack.

## 03-rejected-email

When an event isn't a fit, the organizer always gets the reason, in the moderator's own words. Clear news, the
moment there's news to share.
