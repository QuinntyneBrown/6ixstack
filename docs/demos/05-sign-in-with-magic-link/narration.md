# 05 · Sign in with magic link

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-open-sign-in

Sign in with a magic link lets organizers and moderators get into 6ixstack without a password. Choose Organizer
sign in, and all it asks for is your email.

## 02-check-your-inbox

Enter your address and ask for a link. 6ixstack tells you where it went, that it works once, and that it lasts
fifteen minutes. If it's slow to arrive, you can ask for another after a short wait.

## 03-link-in-mailbox

Here's the email, caught by the development mailbox. It carries a single link, made just for this sign-in.

## 04-signed-in

Open it and you're in. You land on your events, and the header now shows your notifications and your initials,
so you always know you're signed in.

## 05-link-used

Try the same link again and it won't work. It explains what happened, keeps your email ready, and one click sends
a fresh link. One email, one click, and you're in.
