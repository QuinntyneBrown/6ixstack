# 09 · Browse month calendar

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-november

Browse the month calendar to see a whole month of developer events at a glance, no sign-in needed. Today is
marked, each day lists its events in start order, and the line at the top counts what's on.

## 02-next-month

Step forward a month with the arrow. December has nothing listed yet, so the calendar says so, and points you
to submitting an event or following a feed.

## 03-streaming

Back on November with Today, choose Streaming, and the grid keeps only the events you can watch online. The same
filters carry over to the events list.

## 04-phone-day-list

On a phone, each day shows coloured dots instead of titles. Tap a day and its events appear underneath, ready to
open. A month at a glance, wherever you are.
