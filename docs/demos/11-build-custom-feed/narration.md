# 11 · Build custom feed

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-start-a-feed

Build a custom feed when the ready-made ones are too broad. At the bottom of the events page, choose Get a
calendar feed.

## 02-choose-topics

Pick the topics you care about, here AI and Azure, and where you'll be: in the GTA, streaming, or both. If you
forget to pick a topic, it tells you before going any further.

## 03-feed-ready

Create the link and your feed is ready. It says what's in it and how many events match right now. Copy the link,
or add it straight to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

## 04-only-matching-events

Your calendar app gets exactly that mix, and nothing else, kept up to date on its own. Your topics, your
calendar, no more checking.
