## 01-signed-in

When you're signed in, your account and notifications sit at the top right of every page.

## 02-sign-out

To sign out, open Settings and choose Sign out. 6ixstack checks first, and reminds you that your other devices stay
signed in.

## 03-signed-out

Confirm, and you're back on the events page, signed out on this device. Safe on shared computers, simple
everywhere else.
