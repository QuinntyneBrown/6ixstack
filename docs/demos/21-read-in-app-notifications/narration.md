# 21 · Read in-app notifications

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-unread-count

6ixstack keeps you posted while you're signed in. The bell at the top shows how many updates you haven't read yet.

## 02-notifications

Open it to see everything, newest first. Unread updates stand out with a dot, and each one tells you what happened
and when.

## 03-open-one

Choose one to go straight to what it's about. Once you've opened it, it's marked as read.

## 04-all-caught-up

Or mark everything as read in one go, and the bell goes quiet. All caught up.
