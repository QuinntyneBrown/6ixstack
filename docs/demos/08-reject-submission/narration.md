## 01-review-queue

Reject submission keeps things that don't belong off 6ixstack, kindly and clearly. Here's a submission that
already looks wrong: no address, not a developer topic, and a first-time sender.

## 02-reason-required

Choose Reject, and 6ixstack asks why. You can't send it without a reason, because the organizer will see it in
their email.

## 03-reason-and-note

Pick the reason that fits, and add a short note if it helps them understand what would make it acceptable.

## 04-left-the-queue

Reject, and it's done. The card leaves the queue, the organizer is emailed your reason, and you can open the
email to see exactly what they received. A clear reason, a kind note, and the queue moves on.
