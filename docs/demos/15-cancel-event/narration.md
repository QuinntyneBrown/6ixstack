# 15 · Cancel event

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-dashboard

Sometimes an event can't go ahead. Cancel event lets you tell everyone, honestly and quickly. Start on your
dashboard and choose Cancel next to the event.

## 02-give-a-reason

Tell people why in a sentence or two. Your reason shows on the listing, so nobody is left guessing.

## 03-confirm

For a paid event with hundreds of people going, type its name to confirm. It's a small pause before something
that can't be undone.

## 04-cancelled

Confirm, and it's done. The row now says Cancelled, and a note confirms it's marked cancelled on 6ixstack and in
calendar feeds.

## 05-listing-cancelled

The public listing stays up, clearly marked cancelled with your reason, and the ticket link is gone. Plans change;
your attendees will know.
