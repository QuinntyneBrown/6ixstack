# 25 · Manage account settings

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-settings

Settings is where you keep your organizer account up to date: your profile, which emails you get, and the
community you run.

## 02-change-name

Change your name the way you'd like moderators to see it. If you change your email, we send a link to the new
address first, so you never get locked out.

## 03-turn-off-an-email

Choose which updates also arrive by email. Turn one off, and you'll still see it in your notifications on 6ixstack.

## 04-settings-saved

Save, and you'll see exactly when it took effect. Your account, the way you want it.
