One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-communities

The Communities page brings together the meetups and conferences behind every event. Each card tells you where the
group meets, how many people follow it, and what's coming up next.

## 02-search-a-topic

Looking for something specific? Search by name or topic. Type Azure, and you see every group that talks about it.

## 03-city-and-sort

Narrow it to your city, and sort by the next event to see who's meeting soonest.

## 04-no-results

If nothing matches, you'll know right away, with a quick way to clear your search, or to list your own meetup.
Find your people, close to home.
