# 10 · Subscribe to ready-made feed

One paragraph per screenshot, read by Kokoro-82M with the af_heart voice.

## 01-calendar-feeds

Subscribe to a ready-made feed and the GTA's developer events show up in the calendar you already use. The
calendar feeds page lists six feeds, each with what it covers and how many events it holds in the next two weeks.

## 02-ai-feed

Say you only want AI events, local or streamed. That's the AI feed, with its own Copy link button.

## 03-link-copied

Copy the link, and 6ixstack confirms it. Paste it into Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar, and you're
subscribed. How to subscribe walks you through it.

## 04-feed-contents

Behind the link is a standard calendar file your app checks every few hours, with each event in Toronto time.
New, changed and cancelled events update on their own. Subscribe once, and stop checking.
