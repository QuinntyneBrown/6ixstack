# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Source: the HTML mocks in `docs/mocks/` (every page, dialog and notification and
every state listed in `docs/mocks/manifest.json`). Where the mocks rely on an
assumption listed under "Open questions and assumptions" in `docs/mocks/README.md`,
these requirements adopt that assumption.

## L1-001: Event discovery
The system shall let anonymous visitors browse upcoming developer events in Toronto,
the Greater Toronto Area (GTA) and online, grouped by day in Toronto time, with a
featured event, live counts, free-text search, format filters (all, in the GTA,
streaming) and topic filters.

## L1-002: Event map and timeline
The system shall show where upcoming events happen on a map beside the event list,
including where streamed events originate, with a day timeline that can focus on a
single day or play through the next two weeks, kept in sync with the list.

## L1-003: Event detail
The system shall give every published event its own page with full details (date,
time, venue, format, price, attendance, agenda, speakers, venue or stream origin)
and the actions appropriate to its status: upcoming in person, hybrid, streaming,
past or cancelled.

## L1-004: Month calendar
The system shall show events in a month grid with the same format and topic
filters as the event list, with navigation between months.

## L1-005: Communities
The system shall provide a searchable, filterable directory of tech communities and
a page for each community with its description, upcoming and past events and
organizers.

## L1-006: Speakers
The system shall provide a searchable, filterable directory of speakers and a page
for each speaker with a bio, upcoming and past talks and contact links.

## L1-007: Calendar feeds
The system shall let anyone subscribe, without an account, to ready-made or custom
calendar feeds (by topic and format) that calendar applications refresh
automatically.

## L1-008: Add to calendar and share
The system shall let visitors add a single event to their own calendar and share a
link to an event.

## L1-009: Reporting problems with an event
The system shall let anyone report a problem with an event listing to moderators,
anonymously with respect to the organizer.

## L1-010: Event submission
The system shall let anyone submit an event for free, with only an email address,
for moderator review before it is published.

## L1-011: Organizer authentication
The system shall let organizers sign in with an emailed, single-use magic link (no
passwords) and sign out of the current device.

## L1-012: Organizer event management
The system shall let a signed-in organizer see their community's events with review
status and attendance, edit upcoming events, and cancel events with a reason that
is shown to attendees.

## L1-013: Moderation
The system shall provide moderators with a review queue of submitted events,
automated checks for each submission, and the ability to approve (with undo), edit
or reject (with a reason) each one.

## L1-014: Notifications
The system shall notify organizers in the application, and optionally by email,
about review outcomes, reports, and milestones for their events.

## L1-015: Account settings
The system shall let organizers manage their profile and email notification
preferences, see the community they manage, and delete their account.

## L1-016: Static content and contact
The system shall publish an About page and a Code of Conduct, and provide a contact
form that delivers messages to the 6ixstack team.

## L1-017: Status, feedback and error handling
The system shall communicate the outcome of every action and every failure in plain
language through toasts, banners, inline alerts and dedicated not-found, forbidden,
server-error and offline pages, never losing user input on failure.

## L1-018: Time zones
The system shall show every event time in Toronto time (ET) and, for streamed
events, also in the time zone where the stream is hosted.

## L1-019: Security
The system shall protect accounts, data and operations through authentication,
role-based authorization (visitor, organizer, moderator), ownership checks, input
validation, abuse prevention and protection against the OWASP Top 10.

## L1-020: Performance
The system shall load and respond quickly on mobile and desktop connections, and
keep layout stable while data loads.

## L1-021: Accessibility
The system shall conform to WCAG 2.2 level AA, be fully operable by keyboard and
screen reader, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## L1-022: Responsive layout and theming
The system shall adapt to every viewport from extra small (<576px) to extra large
(>=1200px) and support light and dark themes.

## L1-023: Privacy
The system shall collect only the personal data it needs, use no tracking or
advertising cookies, and never expose reporters' or submitters' email addresses
publicly.

## L1-024: Reliability and observability
The system shall log, trace and surface failures so they can be diagnosed, and give
users a reference code they can quote to support.
