6ixstack is one calendar for every developer event in Toronto and the GTA, plus the global talks you can stream. It started in 2025 because finding a good meetup meant checking a dozen Meetup groups, LinkedIn posts and Discord servers.

How listings work

Anyone can submit an event for free. A volunteer moderator checks each one within a day: is it a real event, is it for developers, and are the date, time and venue right? Organizers can then edit their own listings.

What we don’t do

  • We never sell tickets or charge attendees.
  • We don’t track you across sites; there are no ad cookies.
  • We don’t rank events by who pays.

Who runs it

A small group of volunteers from the GTA developer community, led by Jordan Lee. Questions or ideas? Get in touch.
