How it works

  1. Pick a feed — a ready-made one or your own mix of topics.
  2. Add it once to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.
  3. Stop checking. New and changed events show up on their own, in Toronto time.

Feeds are public links with no account needed. Calendar apps refresh them every 2–12 hours.
