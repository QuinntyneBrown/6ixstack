About

We meet on the third Wednesday of every month for two talks, food and a lot of questions. Once a year we run Azure Data & AI Day, a full day of sessions and labs at the Living Arts Centre.

Talks are recorded and posted to our YouTube channel within a week. New speakers are welcome — we pair first-timers with a mentor.
