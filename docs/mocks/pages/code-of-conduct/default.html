Code of conduct

Applies to every event listed on 6ixstack, in person and online.

Every event listed on 6ixstack agrees to provide a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion or nationality.

Expected behaviour

  • Be welcoming to people who are new to the community or to tech.
  • Respect the speaker and the audience; heckling is not debate.
  • Ask before photographing or recording people.

Not acceptable

  • Harassment, intimidation or discrimination in any form.
  • Sexual language or imagery in talks, slides or chat.
  • Sustained disruption of talks or events.

Reporting

Email conduct@6ixstack.dev or choose “Code of conduct concern” under Report a problem on any event page. Reports go to two moderators, never to the event organizer, and we reply within 24 hours.

What happens next

We talk to the people involved, decide on a response — from a warning to removing a group’s listings — and tell you what we did. Organizers who ignore this code lose the ability to list events.
