Azure Data & AI Day GTA

by GTA Azure Community

AzureAI

About this event

A full day of Fabric, Azure OpenAI and data platform sessions from local MVPs, with a hands-on lab track. Bring a laptop with an Azure subscription for the labs; trial credits are available at the door.

  • Two tracks: talks on the main stage, labs in Hall B
  • Lunch and coffee included; vegetarian, vegan and halal options
  • Step-free access from the Square One transit terminal

Agenda

  1. 9:00 AMRegistration and coffee
  2. 9:30 AMKeynote: Fabric one year later — Priya Natarajan
  3. 11:00 AMGrounding Azure OpenAI on your lakehouse — Aisha Rahman
  4. 12:30 PMLunch
  5. 1:30 PMLab: build a real-time dashboard with Eventstream
  6. 4:15 PMPanel: data teams in Canadian banks — Marcus Bell, Kenji Watanabe
