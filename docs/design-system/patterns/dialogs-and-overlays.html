Dialogs and overlays

When 6ixstack interrupts with a dialog, and when a popover, tooltip, menu or a full page does the job better.

Overview

Overlays interrupt, so 6ixstack uses them only when the person has to decide or supply something before continuing, and keeps everything else on the page. The mocks have nine dialogs — add to calendar, subscribe to a feed, share, report, cancel event, reject submission, sign out, delete account and the phone menu — all built from one dialog panel: header (optional status icon, title, description, close), body, sticky footer with the actions. Popovers and tooltips cover the small, non-blocking cases; menus offer a list of actions from one trigger.

Stacking

One dialog at a time. A dialog never opens another dialog; toasts may appear above it (--z-toast > --z-modal) and tooltips above both.

Responsive

  • < sm (640px): the panel is a full-width bottom sheet with --radius-xl top corners; the footer is sticky so actions stay visible above the keyboard.
  • ≥ sm: centred with --space-6 around it and --radius-lg corners; max height is the viewport minus --space-12, and the body scrolls.
  • Footer buttons wrap; when they wrap the primary stays last.
  • Popovers and menus flip to stay inside the viewport; on phones a menu with more than five items becomes a dialog.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA Dialog (Modal): role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby the title, aria-describedby the description. The page behind is inert (the mobile-nav mock wraps it in <div inert aria-hidden="true">).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycles through the dialog's controls only (focus is trapped).
EscCloses the dialog (data-dismiss-on-escape), except while busy.
EnterActivates the focused button; submits a form dialog from a single-line field.

Focus

On open, focus goes to the first field, or to the safe action (“Keep event”, “Keep account”) in destructive dialogs. On close it returns to the control that opened the dialog. Busy dialogs keep focus on the busy button.

Motion

Backdrop fades and the panel scales in (--duration-slow, --ease-enter); bottom sheets slide up. Reduced motion removes both.
