Content and tone

How 6ixstack sounds: voice rules, message formulas, state-by-state labels and a microcopy library taken from the mocks.

Overview

6ixstack sounds like a well-organised friend in the Toronto dev scene: plain, specific, warm without exclamation marks. Copy names real things (the event, the community, the venue, the number of people going) instead of describing the interface. This page collects the voice rules and a microcopy library drawn from the mocks; the content foundation holds the general writing standards (casing, numbers, dates) the system applies everywhere.

  • Specific over generic. “Email the 318 people going” not “Notify attendees”.
  • We, not it. “We couldn't load events” — the site owns its failures; “It's not you.”
  • Local. Times in Toronto time, places in the GTA by name: Toronto, Mississauga, Markham. The 6ix is a nickname used in headlines, not in labels.
  • Short. Buttons ≤ 3 words where possible; one idea per sentence.

Structure

Most messages in the product follow one of three shapes. The live example is an error alert written to the formula.

We couldn't load events

6ixstack didn't respond in time. Your filters are kept; try again in a moment.
Use a calendar feed instead
Title: what failed. Body: why (if known) and what is safe. Actions: retry, then an alternative.
ShapePartsUsed by
Title + body + actionOutcome or problem · consequence or reassurance · verbAlerts, toasts, empty states, dialogs
Label + help + errorNoun · constraint and example · what's wrong and how to fixForm fields
Verb + objectAction · thingButtons, menu items, links

States

The same action reads differently across its states. Keep the verb, change the tense:

IdleBusyDoneFailed
Submit for reviewSubmitting…Thanks, Priya — your event is in reviewWe couldn't submit your event
Send messageSending…Thanks, Priya — message sentWe couldn't send your message
Create feedCreating feed…Your feed is readyWe couldn't create your feed
Save changesSaving…Settings saved · Saved at 10:42 AMWe couldn't save your settings
Copy link—CopiedCouldn't copy the link

Responsive

  • Write for 360px first: a button label that wraps there is too long; a title that wraps to three lines needs cutting.
  • The header's “Submit event” keeps its full text as the accessible name when it collapses to an icon on phones.
  • Hero copy uses text-wrap: balance; never insert manual line breaks to shape it.

Accessibility

  • Link text makes sense alone (2.4.4): “Use a calendar feed instead”, “Get a feed for Kubernetes”, never “click here”.
  • Repeated actions add the object in visually hidden text: “Reject Rust in production at scale”.
  • Errors name the field and the fix (3.3.3). Required fields are the default; optional ones say “(optional)”.
  • Plain language: no jargon in public pages; organizer pages may say “listing”, “feed” and “moderator”, which the about page defines.
  • Numbers that carry meaning are written into labels: aria-label="142 going", “3 unread”.
