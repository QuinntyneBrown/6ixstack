Notifications

How 6ixstack tells people what happened: toasts for the moment, banners for lasting conditions, and the notification list as history — with priority, grouping and dismissal rules.

Overview

6ixstack tells people about things that happen to their events (approved, reported, needs changes, milestones) and about the state of the site (offline, unverified email). Three surfaces carry these messages, ranked by how long they matter: a toast for the moment, a banner or inline alert for as long as a condition lasts, and the notification list as the durable history. Email mirrors the list for organizers who opt in under Settings → Email notifications.

States

Notification items are unread or read; “Mark all as read” moves every item to read without removing it (notifications/read). Banners and alerts come in info, success, warning and danger, plus persistent (no dismiss) for conditions the person cannot dismiss away.

Priority and grouping

  1. Danger (needs changes, rejected, couldn't save) — persistent; toasts use role="alert".
  2. Warning (reported, offline, unverified) — persistent until resolved.
  3. Success (live, saved, added) — auto-dismiss.
  4. Info (milestones, follower counts) — list only, no toast.

At most three toasts show at once; older ones collapse into the notification history (app-toast/stacked). Repeated events about one listing (several reports) group into one notification with a count.

Responsive

  • Notification items put the time on its own line below sm and to the right above it.
  • Site banners wrap title, text and action onto separate lines on phones.
  • Toasts follow feedback and loading: full width on phones, 24rem bottom right above sm.

Accessibility

Unread state is not colour-only: the dot is paired with a semibold title and visually hidden “(unread)” text. The header badge has a label with meaning (“3 unread notifications”). Banners are role="status"; only new danger toasts use role="alert". Nothing auto-dismisses that requires action (2.2.1).
