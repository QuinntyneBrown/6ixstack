Radio group

Radios pick exactly one option from a short visible list: event format, free or paid, how often a feed updates.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative radios in <fieldset>

Overview

Use radios for two to five mutually exclusive options that should all be visible. Use a select for longer lists, and a segmented control when the choice filters content in place rather than being submitted.

Anatomy

  1. LegendNames the question.
  2. Radio20px circle; ink fill and white dot when checked.
  3. Label and descriptionOption and what it means.
  4. Card (card style)Selected card gets an accent border.

Variants

Price
Horizontal (.choice-row) for two short options.
HTML
<fieldset><legend>Price</legend><div class="choice-row"><label class="choice"><input type="radio" name="p" checked><span class="choice__text">Free</span></label><label class="choice"><input type="radio" name="p"><span class="choice__text">Paid</span></label></div></fieldset>
Feed updates
Vertical with descriptions.
HTML
<fieldset><legend>Feed updates</legend><div class="stack stack--sm"><label class="choice"><input type="radio" name="u" checked><span class="choice__text">Every hour<small>Recommended for Google Calendar.</small></span></label><label class="choice"><input type="radio" name="u"><span class="choice__text">Every day</span></label></div></fieldset>
Format
Card style (.choice-cards) for options that need a description and a larger target.
HTML
<fieldset style="width: 100%;"><legend>Format</legend><div class="choice-cards"><label class="choice-card choice"><input type="radio" name="v" checked><span class="choice__text">In person<small>At a venue in the GTA</small></span></label><label class="choice-card choice"><input type="radio" name="v"><span class="choice__text">Streaming<small>Online only</small></span></label><label class="choice-card choice"><input type="radio" name="v"><span class="choice__text">Hybrid<small>Venue plus a livestream</small></span></label></div></fieldset>

Sizes

One size: a 20px circle in a 24px row. Card style adds --space-4 padding.

States

Responsive behaviour

  • Choice cards are three columns from 640px and stack below.
  • Horizontal rows wrap with --space-5 between options.

Theming

Same tokens as the checkbox; cards add --color-border-default at rest and --color-accent as a 2px edge when selected.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native radios sharing a name, in a <fieldset> with <legend> (APG Radio Group behaviour comes free).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves into the group (to the checked radio) and out of it.
↓ → / ↑ ←Selects the next / previous option.
SpaceSelects the focused option if none is checked.

Focus

Ring around the circle; on cards, around the circle inside the card.

Labelling

The legend asks the question; each label answers it. Descriptions are inside the label so they are read with it.

Contrast

Radio border
Selected card edge
Description
Focus ring on surface

Motion

Fill transition only.

Touch

Cards are at least 64px tall; the whole card is the label.

Content

  • Legends are short questions or nouns: “Format”, “Price”.
  • Options are parallel: “In person / Streaming / Hybrid”.
  • Pre-select the most common option.
