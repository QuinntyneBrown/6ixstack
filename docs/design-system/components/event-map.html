Event map

The event map is 6ixstack’s signature: an always-ink panel showing where the next two weeks happen across the GTA, which streams arrive from around the world, and a timeline to play them day by day.

Overview

The map answers “where is it happening?” at a glance. It sits beside the event list on wide screens (sticky, full height) and above it on phones. It is a companion to the list, never a replacement: everything on the map is also in the list as text.

  • Dots are in-person events, filled in the topic hue; rings are hybrid; arcs with moving comets are streams flowing into Toronto from their origin (Redmond, Seattle, Mountain View).
  • The timeline lists the next 14 days; each day button shows one dot per event and filters the map and list to that day. Play steps through the days automatically.
  • The compact variant on an event page shows one venue with a tooltip and no timeline.

Anatomy

  1. HeadTitle (live region), subtitle with counts, legend of dot / ring / line.
  2. TooltipWhite SVG card with title and time, pointing at the hot mark.
  3. Venue markTopic-hue dot (in person) with a dashed ring for hybrid and a ripple when hot.
  4. Land and lakeDot-pattern fills, city labels in mono caps, “Lake Ontario” in italics.
  5. Panel--color-map-bg in both themes, --radius-xl, 1px map border.

Sizes

Two sizes by variant: the full map is 100dvh − 7.5rem tall (min 600px) from 1024px and 300px tall above the list on smaller screens; the compact map is 280px tall at every width.

States

–

Map-level states: default (all marks), day focus (other days’ marks dim via .is-dim/.is-off, “Show all dates” appears), playing (the current day carries data-state="playing", the title announces each day), loading (skeleton tiles on map controls) and notice (.event-map__notice when no events match).

Both themes

The panel is ink in both themes; the page around it changes. The topic hues switch to their bright primitives inside .event-map in both themes.

Responsive

  • Below 1024px the map sits above the list, 300px tall, and the timeline stays under it; day buttons share the width (14 columns of at least 22px with 4px gaps).
  • From 1024px it moves to a sticky right column (.page-grid--map) and fills the viewport height.
  • The SVG uses preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid meet", so Toronto stays centred at any aspect ratio.

Theming

The map has its own semantic token family (--color-map-*) so it stays ink regardless of theme; only --color-map-bg and --color-map-border shift slightly in dark theme to sit on the darker canvas. Map controls use opaque tile tokens (--color-map-control, --color-map-control-hover) so their contrast is measurable. Motion durations are tokens (--duration-map-ripple, --duration-map-stream, --duration-map-step).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The SVG is a single image: role="img" with an aria-label that summarises it (“Map of 9 events across the GTA and 5 streams arriving from around the world”). Marks are not focusable; the list beside it is the accessible equivalent. Day buttons are APG toggle buttons (aria-pressed) in role="group" aria-label="Choose a day".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabPlay, “Show all dates” (when shown), then each enabled day button.
Space, EnterToggles a day (focus the map and list on it) or starts/stops playback.
EscWhile a day is focused: shows all dates again.

Focus

Inside the map the focus ring switches to --color-map-fg (white) so it is visible on ink.

Labelling

Each day button’s label is the full date and count: “Thursday, November 5: 2 events”; letters, numbers and dots inside are hidden. The map title is aria-live="polite", so choosing or playing a day announces “Thursday, November 5”. Keep the Play button’s label constant and use aria-pressed for the playing state.

City labels (--color-map-fg-faint) are decorative geography inside the image and exempt; the image’s label carries the meaning.

Motion

Comets travel each arc (SMIL animateMotion, 3s), venue dots ripple (--duration-map-ripple), and playback steps every --duration-map-step. Under prefers-reduced-motion comets and ripples are hidden and static .event-map__comet-still dots show the direction instead; Play still works but jumps day to day. Playback stops when focus leaves the timeline (WCAG 2.2.2).

Touch

Day buttons are 44px tall; Play is 28px tall but wide, with the timeline’s padding around it.

Content

  • Title: “Where it’s happening”, or the chosen day (“Thursday, November 5”), or the venue on the compact map.
  • Subtitle: “9 across the GTA · 5 streaming in”. Write “No streams” rather than “0 streaming in”.
  • Stream origins: city and local time in mono caps: “Redmond · 9:00 AM PT”.
  • The SVG label must use correct plurals: “1 event”, not “1 events”.
