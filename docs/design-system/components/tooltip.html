Tooltip

A tooltip shows the name of an icon-only control on hover and on keyboard focus.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAPG TooltipText only

Overview

Every icon-only button in 6ixstack (notifications, close, copy, share, more actions) has a tooltip that repeats its name for sighted mouse and keyboard users. Tooltips never hold links, buttons or anything a person needs to read to complete a task; use a popover for that, and visible helper text for form hints.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerIcon-only button with aria-label; .has-tooltip makes it the positioning context.
  2. Bubble--color-bg-inverse, --text-caption, --radius-sm, 4×8px padding.
  3. Arrow5px CSS triangle pointing at the trigger.

Sizes

One size: caption text on one line; tooltips never wrap.

States

VariantHidden (default)Visible on hoverVisible on focus
Icon button

In the product the bubble appears on :hover and :focus-within; data-state="hover" forces it for review. The forced-focus example shows the ring; its bubble appears with real keyboard focus.

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • Tooltips sit above the trigger at every breakpoint. Near the top of the viewport (the sticky header) the CDK overlay flips them below.
  • On touch screens there is no hover; the icon's aria-label is the name, and a long-press shows the bubble only where the platform supports it. Never rely on a tooltip for touch users.

Theming

Inverse colours: --color-bg-inverse with --color-fg-inverse. In the dark theme they swap (light bubble, ink text), so the tooltip always contrasts with the surface it floats over.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Tooltip: role="tooltip". When the tooltip repeats the accessible name (the common case: “Close”), the button keeps its aria-label and the tooltip is not referenced, so the name is not read twice. When it adds information, link it with aria-describedby.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocusing the trigger shows the tooltip.
EscHides the visible tooltip without moving focus (WCAG 1.4.13).

Focus

The trigger shows the shared ring; the tooltip itself is never focusable and has pointer-events: none.

Labelling

The tooltip text equals or shortens the aria-label: “Notifications” for “Notifications, 3 unread”.

Contrast

Tooltip text

Motion

Fades in over --duration-fast after a 300ms hover delay, out with no delay; reduced motion removes the fade.

Touch

Not available on touch; the control must make sense without it.

Content

  • The control's name, sentence case, no full stop: “Copy link”.
  • Two to three words. Anything longer is helper text or a popover.
