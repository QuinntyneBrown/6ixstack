Stepper

A stepper shows where you are in a task split into ordered steps; the next-steps list shows what happens after you finish.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: core<ol> + aria-current="step"

Overview

A stepper shows progress through a task split into ordered steps, and which step is current. It is a status display, not navigation: completed steps can be links back, future steps are not.

  • Use it when a task has three to five steps that must happen in order and each step is its own screen.
  • Do not use it on the Submit event form today: the mock is one long form with four form-sections on one page, which is easier to review and needs no stepper. Use section headings and an error summary instead (see form layout).
  • For "what happens next" after a task (the numbered list on the submit-event success page), use the .steps-next list, which documents future steps without implying progress.

Anatomy

  1. Completed stepCheck mark on a --color-success-fg disc; a visually hidden "(completed)".
  2. Current stepFilled ink disc with its number, label in accent colour, aria-current="step".
  3. Upcoming stepNumbered ring in --color-border-strong, muted label.

Variants

  1. Event basics (completed)
  2. Where (completed)
  3. Details
  4. Review
Horizontal: above a multi-screen task.

Responsive

  • Below 768px: only the current step's label is shown; the other steps show their markers only (labels stay in the accessibility tree via the visually hidden text). Add "Step 2 of 4" in the page heading.
  • 768px and up: all labels are shown; steps share the width equally.
  • Use the vertical variant in a side column at 1024px and up when steps have descriptions.

Theming

Reads --color-border-strong (upcoming ring), --color-accent / --color-fg-on-accent (current), --color-success-fg / --color-fg-inverse (complete), --color-danger-solid / --color-fg-on-danger (error) and --color-fg-muted labels.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An ordered list (<ol>) with an aria-label; the current step has aria-current="step". There is no APG widget; it is static structure.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the stepper unless completed steps are links back.
EnterFollows a link back to a completed step.

Focus

Focus moves to the new step's heading after Continue, not to the stepper.

Labelling

Status that is shown by colour or icon is also in text: "(completed)" and "(has errors)" are visually hidden spans. The page title includes "Step 2 of 4".

Contrast

Upcoming ring on surface
Current number on accent
Complete mark on success disc
Step label on surface

Motion

None.

Touch

Not a touch target unless steps are links; then the whole step label is the link and at least 24px tall.

Content

  • Step labels are short nouns that match the step's heading: "Event basics", "Where", "Details", "Review".
  • Three to five steps; more means the task should be simplified.
  • Next-steps lists use bold outcome lines with one sentence of detail each.
