Form layout

How fields are arranged into forms: sections with headings, one- and two-column grids, the actions bar and the error summary.

Overview

6ixstack's long forms (submit event, edit event, settings) are a .form-card holding .form-sections, each with a heading, one sentence of context and a .form-grid. Short forms (sign-in, contact, dialogs) are a single grid. All end in .form-actions. See the forms pattern for validation timing.

Anatomy

  1. Form cardSurface with --space-6 padding.
  2. Section heading and context--text-h4 + muted body-sm.
  3. GridOne column; two or three from 640px; --space-5 gap.
  4. ActionsRight-aligned, primary last, --space-3 gap.

Sizes

Long forms sit in .page--narrow; dialogs give forms their panel width.

States

A form is idle, submitting, invalid, failed or succeeded.

VariantIdleSubmittingInvalidFailed
Actions bar

Message not sent

Check your connection and try again.
  • Submitting: the primary is busy, the fieldset is disabled, a live region says “Sending your message…”.
  • Invalid: the summary appears, takes focus and lists each error as a link.
  • Failed (server): a danger alert above the actions; the values are kept.
  • Success: a success panel replaces the form, or the page navigates and a toast confirms.

Responsive behaviour

  • Grids are one column below 640px and two or three above; never use two columns for unrelated fields.
  • Below 768px the actions bar stacks: primary on top and full width.
  • On long forms on phones the primary may move into a sticky bottom bar.

Theming

Reads --color-bg-surface (card), --color-border-default (section dividers) and the alert danger tokens for the summary.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <form novalidate> with custom messages; sections are <section aria-labelledby>; groups are <fieldset>. The summary is role="alert" with tabindex="-1".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through fields in visual order.
EnterIn a text field: submits the form.
Enter on a summary linkMoves focus to that field.

Focus

On a failed submit, focus moves to the summary; its links move focus to fields. After success, focus moves to the success panel heading.

Labelling

Section headings name groups of fields; the summary title counts the errors (“Fix 3 things before you continue”).

Content

  • Section headings are nouns: “Event basics”, “When”, “Where”, “Tickets”.
  • One sentence of context under each heading when it helps.
  • Summary title counts: “Fix 2 things before you continue”.
  • The primary action names the outcome: “Submit for review”, “Save changes”, “Send message”.
