Notification item

Notification items are the entries on the notifications page: what happened to an event you submitted or follow, when, and a link to it.

Overview

Use notification items for persistent, per-person history: approvals, requests for changes, cancellations, digests. For a message about something the person just did, use a toast; for a site-wide notice use a banner.

Anatomy

  1. Unread dot6px brand dot at the left edge; decorative, paired with hidden “(unread)” text and a bolder title.
  2. Tone icon40px circle in the status tone: success, warning, danger, or neutral.
  3. Title linkStretched over the item; semibold when unread, muted when read.
  4. TextOne or two sentences of detail.
  5. TimeRelative (“12 min ago”) for today, then “Yesterday”, then a date.

Responsive

  • Below 640px the time moves under the text (second column, last row).
  • From 640px the time sits in a third column at the top right.
  • Titles wrap; they never truncate.

Theming

The list uses surface and default border; tone circles use --color-*-bg with --color-*-icon. The unread dot is --color-brand in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <ul> of items, each with one title link stretched over the item (block-link technique). Unread state is conveyed three ways: hidden “(unread)” text, a heavier title, and the dot.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabOne stop per item (the title link), after “Mark all as read”.
EnterOpens the event and marks the item read.

Focus

Shared ring on the link; because the link’s hit area covers the item, the ring is drawn on the text. Keep focus on the next item after “Mark all as read”.

Labelling

Icons are hidden; the tone is in the words (“is live”, “needs changes”, “was cancelled”). The page heading’s lead (“3 unread”) and the header bell label update together.

Motion

None; marking as read changes weight and removes the dot instantly.

Touch

The whole item (at least 72px tall) is the target.

Content

  • Title: quote the event name, then what happened: ““Azure Bicep clinic” needs changes”.
  • Text: who did it and what to do next: “A moderator asked you to add the venue address…”.
  • Time: “12 min ago”, “2 h ago”, “Yesterday”, then “Oct 26”.
