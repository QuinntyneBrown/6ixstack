Sidebar navigation

Sidebar navigation moves between the sections of one area, such as the four parts of Settings, and shows where you are.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<nav> landmark

Overview

Sidebar navigation moves between the sections of one screen or one area: the four parts of Settings (Profile, Email notifications, Community, Account) today, and the organizer area as it grows. It is a vertical list of .nav-links that becomes a horizontally scrolling row on phones and tablets.

  • Use it when a screen has three to eight peer sections a person moves between and comes back to.
  • Use the site header for the four public destinations; never repeat them in a sidebar.
  • Use tabs when the sections are views of the same object that swap in place without scrolling, and a segmented button group to filter one list (Upcoming / Past on Events).

Anatomy

  1. Containernav.settings-nav with a label naming the area ("Settings sections"); 13rem column at 1024px and up, sticky 5rem from the top.
  2. Current itemaria-current on the link; --color-bg-subtle fill and default ink.
  3. Item.nav-link: 44px tall, --space-3 padding, optional 16px leading icon, sentence-case label.

Variants

Two variants. Section nav is the settings pattern from the mocks: in-page anchors, no groups. Grouped sidebar (.sidebar + .sidebar__group) is the core-set variant for an area with more than six destinations, such as the organizer tools, with optional count badges.

Both themes

ProfileEmail notificationsCommunityAccount
  • Current is aria-current: "page" when each item is its own URL, "true" (or "location") when items are anchors inside one page, as in settings.
  • Disabled items are not shown. If a section needs a permission the person lacks, leave it out and explain on the page they land on (see review-queue/forbidden).
  • Expanded applies only to the grouped sidebar on phones, where it lives in a drawer behind a menu button with aria-expanded.

Responsive

  • Below 1024px (--layout-breakpoint-lg): the section nav is a single horizontal row above the content, scrolling sideways when it overflows (no wrap, no scrollbar chrome). The current item scrolls into view on load.
  • 1024px and up: it becomes a 13rem column beside the content (.settings-layout) and sticks 5rem from the top, just under the 64px site header.
  • The grouped sidebar follows the shell rule: visible as a column at 1024px and up, otherwise hidden and opened as a drawer from a menu button (.sidebar[data-open="true"]).
  • Gap between the nav and content is --space-8 at every width.

Theming

Items read the shared .nav-link tokens: --color-fg-muted label, --color-bg-subtle hover and current fill, --color-fg-default current label. In dark theme the subtle fill becomes a raised grey (#24262d) that still reads against the surface. Group labels use --text-overline in --color-fg-subtle.

/* Give an area a stronger current marker without forking the component */
.settings-nav .nav-link[aria-current] { background: var(--color-accent-subtle); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <nav> landmark with a unique aria-label containing plain links: a navigation landmark, not an ARIA menu or tablist. Grouped variants put group labels in plain text above their links; when groups collapse they use <details> or the APG Disclosure pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the items in order.
EnterFollows the link; for in-page sections, moves focus to the section heading.
Arrow keysNo effect; links keep standard Tab behaviour.

Focus

Shared focus ring. When an in-page item is activated, the target section (with tabindex="-1") receives focus and scroll-margin-top keeps its heading clear of the sticky header.

Labelling

Label the landmark with what it navigates ("Settings sections", "Organizer"), never "Sidebar". Count badges carry their meaning in aria-label ("4 waiting"). Icons are aria-hidden.

Contrast

Item label on surface
Current label on subtle fill
Group label on surface
Focus ring on surface

Motion

Hover and current fills transition over --duration-fast; no movement. Smooth scrolling to anchors is turned off under reduced motion.

Touch

Items are 44px tall and full width in the column, so the whole row is the target. In the horizontal row each item is at least 44px tall with 4px between items.

Content

  • Labels name the section as a noun phrase in sentence case: "Email notifications", not "Notification Settings" or "Manage emails".
  • Match each label to the heading of the section it opens, word for word.
  • Order by frequency of use, with destructive or rare sections (Account, which holds delete account) last.
  • Keep labels to three words so the horizontal row on phones shows at least two items.
