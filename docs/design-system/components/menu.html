Menu

A button that opens a short list of actions: the account menu in the header and the More actions menu on an organizer's event.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAPG Menu Button

Overview

Use a menu when one trigger offers three to seven related actions and showing them all would crowd the surface: Account (Settings, My events, Sign out) and an organizer's per-event More actions (Edit, Duplicate, Cancel event). Use buttons when there are one or two actions; use a select to choose a value; use navigation links for moving between pages in the header.

The mocks do not open a menu yet; this component is part of the core set and its classes ship in components.css.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerA button with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.
  2. PanelRaised surface, --radius-md, --shadow-3, --space-1 padding, min 12rem.
  3. Item32px tall, icon + label + optional shortcut.
  4. Divider and danger itemDestructive items sit last, after a divider, in danger text.

Responsive behaviour

  • The panel opens below and aligned to the trigger's start edge; it flips to open upward or end-aligned when there is no room.
  • Below 640px a menu with more than five items opens as a bottom drawer instead.
  • Items never truncate; the panel grows to 90vw max.

Theming

Reads --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-default, --shadow-3, --color-bg-subtle (item hover) and --color-danger-fg. In dark the raised surface is lighter than the page and the shadow darker, so the panel still lifts.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The APG Menu Button pattern: trigger with aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded, aria-controls; panel role="menu"; items menuitem, menuitemradio or menuitemcheckbox.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter, Space, ↓On the trigger: opens and focuses the first item.
↑On the trigger: opens and focuses the last item.
↓ / ↑Moves between items, wrapping.
Home / EndFirst / last item.
Type a letterMoves to the next item starting with it.
EnterActivates the item and closes.
EscCloses and returns focus to the trigger.
TabCloses and moves on.

Focus

Focus moves into the menu on open (roving focus on items, not the ring on the panel) and back to the trigger on close. Items show the subtle hover fill as their focus indicator.

Labelling

The trigger's text or aria-label names the menu (“More actions for Fabric for Power BI developers”). Disabled items keep aria-disabled so they stay discoverable.

Contrast

Motion

The panel scales in from 96% over --duration-fast with --ease-enter; reduced motion shows it instantly.

Touch

Items are 32px tall with full-width hit areas; on touch screens long menus become a drawer with 44px rows.

Content

  • Items are verb phrases in sentence case: “Edit details”, “Cancel event”, “Sign out”.
  • Destructive items are last and open a confirmation.
  • No more than seven items; group with a label when there are two kinds.
