Popover

A popover opens a small, non-modal panel next to its trigger for a short explanation or a quick choice that does not block the page.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableDisclosure (non-modal dialog)Not yet used in the mocks

Overview

Use a popover for content that is too long for a tooltip or that contains a control: why times are shown in Toronto time, a short legend, a two-option choice. Use a menu for a list of actions and a dialog when the person must answer before continuing.

No mock uses a popover yet; the event-map legend and the “Toronto time” note are the first candidates.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerAny button with aria-haspopup="dialog" and aria-expanded.
  2. PanelRaised surface, --radius-md, --shadow-3, max 20rem.
  3. Title--text-label; names the panel.
  4. Actions (optional)Right-aligned small buttons.

Responsive behaviour

  • The panel opens below and left-aligned to the trigger; near the right edge it flips to right-aligned (Angular CDK Overlay handles the flip).
  • Below 640px, a popover with a form becomes a bottom-sheet dialog; a text popover stays a popover capped at 90vw.

Theming

Reads --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-default and --shadow-3; the border keeps the edge visible in the dark theme where shadows are weak.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A disclosure: a button with aria-haspopup="dialog", aria-expanded and aria-controls toggling a non-modal role="dialog" labelled by its title. Not a tooltip: it opens on click, not hover. See APG Disclosure.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter / SpaceOpens or closes the popover from the trigger.
EscCloses and returns focus to the trigger.
TabMoves into the popover's actions when it has any; otherwise past it. Tabbing out closes it.

Focus

Focus stays on the trigger for text-only popovers; it moves to the first control when the popover has actions.

Labelling

The title is the dialog's name. Keep the trigger label meaningful on its own (“Toronto time”, not “i”).

Contrast

Title on panel
Caption on panel

Motion

No animation beyond the CDK overlay fade; nothing moves under reduced motion.

Touch

Triggers are at least 32px tall; the panel closes on an outside tap.

Content

  • Title: the fact, as a sentence (“Times are in Toronto time”).
  • Body: one or two sentences; link out for more.
  • Action: “Got it” only when the popover was opened automatically.
