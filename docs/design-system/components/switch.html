Switch

A switch turns a setting on or off immediately: which emails 6ixstack sends you, whether your profile shows your events.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="switch" on a checkbox

Overview

Use a switch for a setting that takes effect the moment it changes, with a toast confirming “Settings saved”. If the change waits for a Save button, use a checkbox. The label describes the setting (“My event is approved or needs changes”), not the action (“Turn on emails”).

Anatomy

  1. Track44 × 24px, --radius-full; strong border colour off, accent on.
  2. Thumb20px, surface colour, --shadow-1; slides 20px.
  3. Label and descriptionThe setting, in sentence case.

Responsive behaviour

  • In settings rows the switch sits at the row end from 640px; below it stays after the label and the label wraps.

Theming

Track off reads --color-border-strong, on reads --color-accent; the thumb is --color-bg-surface. In dark the on track is off-white with a dark thumb.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native checkbox with role="switch" (APG Switch); screen readers announce “on/off”.

Keyboard

KeyAction

SpaceToggles and saves.

Focus

Ring around the track.

Labelling

The visible label names the setting; the state is announced by the role. Saving announces through the toast region.

Motion

The thumb slides over --duration-base with --ease-standard; reduced motion jumps.

Touch

The label is part of the target; rows are at least 24px.

Content

  • Describe the setting as a statement that is true when on: “Someone reports my event”.
  • Never “On/Off” text beside the switch; the track shows it.
