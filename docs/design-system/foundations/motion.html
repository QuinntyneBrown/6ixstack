Motion

Five durations, four easings and three status loops, all zeroed by the tokens when the reader prefers reduced motion.

WCAG 2.2 AASource: tokens/tokens.css

Overview

Motion in 6ixstack is functional: it confirms a press, shows where a dialog came from, and says something is loading or live. Five durations and four easings cover every transition; three loop durations drive the spinner, skeleton shimmer and the live-now pulse.

Durations

Press a button to replay the transition at that duration.

TokenValueUsed for
--duration-instantState flips with no transition
--duration-fastHover, colour and border changes, button press
--duration-baseMenus, tooltips, day-focus dimming in the event list
--duration-slowDialogs, sheets, mobile nav
--duration-deliberateToast progress, map arc drawing

Event map motion

The ink event map has four motion tokens of its own. They are slow on purpose: the map is ambient context beside the list, and it must never pull the eye away from reading.

TokenValueUsed for
--duration-map-rippleevent map: ripple from each GTA dot
--duration-map-streamevent map: comet trip along a stream arc (mirrored in SMIL)
--duration-map-stream-hoverStream arc comet when its event is hovered or focused in the list
--duration-map-stepevent map: “Play the next two weeks” step

Under reduced motion the ripples and moving comets are hidden and a still comet marks each stream arc instead (the stream comets are SMIL, so components.css swaps them rather than relying on the token); the map still shows every dot, ring and arc.

Usage

  • Enter slower than you exit: a dialog opens over --duration-slow and closes over --duration-base.
  • Animate opacity and transform/translate only; never animate layout properties.
  • The map “Play the next two weeks” timeline steps a day at a time and is pausable; it never autoplays.
  • No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no motion that loops forever except the three status loops.

Accessibility

  • Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the tokens set every duration except instant and spin to 0.01ms, so components need no media queries of their own; components.css also clamps any remaining animation to one iteration.
  • Nothing flashes more than three times a second (2.3.1).
  • The map timeline animation can be paused with a visible button and the keyboard (2.2.2).
