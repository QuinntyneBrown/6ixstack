Overview

6ixstack sounds like a well-organised friend in the Toronto dev scene: specific, warm, brief. It names the event, the place and the time; it tells people what happens next; it never markets at them. Copy below is taken from the mocks.

Voice and tone

PrincipleSounds likeNot like
Specific“Living Arts Centre, Mississauga · Livestream available”“Great venue, also online!”
Local“Every dev event in the 6ix.” · “Times are in Toronto time (ET)”“Events near you”
Reassuring when things fail“6ixstack didn't respond in time. Your filters are kept; try again in a moment.”“Error 504: request failed”
Honest about what we do“Tickets are sold by the organizer. 6ixstack never charges attendees.”“Buy now”
Human“Questions, corrections or ideas — a person reads every message.”“Submit a ticket”

Casing and punctuation

  • Sentence case everywhere: titles, buttons, labels, menu items (“Add to my calendar”, “Calendar feeds”). Proper nouns keep their case: “.NET”, “Azure OpenAI”, “GTA”.
  • No full stop on buttons, labels, titles or single-sentence toasts; full stops in help text and multi-sentence descriptions.
  • Middle dot ( · ) separates metadata: “Thu, Nov 5 · 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM ET”. En dash for ranges.
  • Curly quotes and apostrophes in copy (“Where it’s happening”); straight quotes only in code.
  • Uppercase is a CSS treatment for overlines only; never type in capitals.

Buttons and links

  • Lead with a verb that says what happens: “Submit an event”, “Add to my calendar”, “Get tickets · $49”, “Send message”, “Save settings”, “Try again”, “Clear filters”.
  • Name the object when the context doesn't: “Cancel event” in the dialog footer, “Edit” in a table row whose name is in the accessible label.
  • Destructive buttons name the loss: “Cancel event”, “Delete account”, “Reject submission”; the safe action is “Keep event”, not “No”.
  • Links say where they go: “Browse the calendar”, “Use a calendar feed instead”, “Watch the recording”.

Error message formula

What happened (title) + why, if known + what is safe + what to do (one action).

SituationCopy
Load failureWe couldn't load events — 6ixstack didn't respond in time. Your filters are kept; try again in a moment. [Try again] [Use a calendar feed instead]
Dialog action failedWe couldn't create the calendar file — Try again, or add the event to your calendar from its page.
FieldEnter an email address like name@example.com
FieldEnd time must be after the 6:30 PM start.
SummaryFix 3 things before you continue
ForbiddenYou don't have access to this page — Only moderators can review submissions.
Not foundWe can't find that page — The event may have been removed by its organizer, or the link has a typo.

Field errors say how to fix, not what is wrong (“Pick at least one topic so people can find it”, not “Topic is required”). Never blame the person; never show raw codes except the 404/403/500 numeral on status pages.

Inclusive language

  • Use “people” and “attendees”, not “users”.
  • Speakers and organizers are named as they name themselves; never guess pronouns.
  • Avoid idioms that don't translate (“knock it out of the park”); many attendees are newcomers to Canada.
  • Accessibility details of venues (step-free access, quiet room) are stated plainly in the event's facts.
